- **The kit begins** with four libraries: `PairingLink` (the pairing link, pins and pin sets, the trust decision, a server's reach), `ServerAddresses` (a machine's LAN, tailnet and `.local` addresses), `ServerIdentity` (a server's own self-signed TLS identity, or a supplied one, pinned by its SubjectPublicKeyInfo and its whole certificate) and `ServerPairing` (one-time codes, the claim throttle, device sessions).
