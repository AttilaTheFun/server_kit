- **The kit begins** with five libraries: `NetworkCore` (a machine's networks, IPv4 and IPv6, no Foundation), `PairingLink` (the pairing link with its credential, paths and an app's own fields; pins and pin sets; the trust decision; a server's reach), `ServerAddresses` (a server's LAN, tailnet and `.local` addresses), `ServerIdentity` (a server's own self-signed TLS identity, or a supplied one, pinned by its SubjectPublicKeyInfo and its whole certificate, kept in one file written whole) and `ServerPairing` (one-time codes, the claim throttle, device sessions).
