- **ServerTLS**, a new library: `TLSFront` ends TLS on every interface (SwiftNIO; IPv6 and IPv4, or IPv4's alone where there's no IPv6) with the pair `ServerCertificates` picks by the hello's SNI (a CA's for its names and suffixes, the server's own for the rest, an IP or no name included), and hands each connection to the server's plain listener on loopback behind `SERVERKIT-TLS <secret>` and a PROXY v1 line (`ProxyV1`, with its parser: the secret compared in constant time, addresses checked by family, ports in range). Back-pressure both ways, 256 connections at once, a 5-minute idle limit and 1 MB held before the inner listener answers, each adjustable; new certificates taken up by `use` without a restart. `TailscaleCertificate` asks `tailscale cert` for a tailnet's pair and keeps it (the key 0600).
