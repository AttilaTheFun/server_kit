- **A tailnet's IPv6 clients count one by one** (Visor's catch). ClaimThrottle and TLSFront's `maxPerAddress` keyed every IPv6 client by its /64, but a tailnet's nodes all share one /96 (`fd7a:115c:a1e0::/48`, Tailscale's), so one bad node locked out every other, the owner's phone included. A tailnet address now counts alone, as the tailnet assigns it per node; any other IPv6 address still by its /64. New in NetworkCore: `IPv6.isTailnet` and `IPv6.clientKey`.
